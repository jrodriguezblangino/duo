# GTM Argentina — Outreach Strategy for the Metal-Siding Website Mockup

**Goal:** Use the high-end mockup (`/docs/build-plan.md` done) as a *Trojan Horse* to land
website-building contracts with mid-size metal-siding importers in Argentina (mostly importing
from China / Asia, aiming to scale).

**Mental model:** You are NOT selling "a website." You are selling *a version of their company
that already looks like the market leader.* The mockup is the proof. Prospects don't hire you
because of a portfolio PDF — they hire you because they **saw their own future brand** for 60
seconds and wanted it.

**Tone rule (Argentina / Rioplatense):** Warm, peer-to-peer, low-pressure. Never a cold "pitch."
Lead with a genuine compliment on *their* work, offer value, ask for nothing heavy up front.
WhatsApp and IG DM are the channels — email is for the contract stage only.

---

## 1. Who you are targeting

| Trait | Profile |
|-------|---------|
| **Business** | Importers/distributors of metal cladding (aluminium composite, steel, wood-look panels) |
| **Origin** | Mostly Asia (China) → Argentina; some Europe |
| **Size** | Mid-size, 10–60 employees, owner/founder-led or 2nd generation |
| **Pain** | Strong product, weak digital presence; can't communicate quality/trust online |
| **Decision-maker** | Owner, commercial manager, or marketing spouse — reachable on WA/IG |
| **Where they live online** | Instagram (showroom), WhatsApp (sales), sometimes LinkedIn (B2B) |

**Why this works:** They already import a premium product but present it like a 2015 catalog.
Your mockup makes the gap — and the upside — visible in one scroll.

## 3. Channels — when to use each

| Channel | Use for | When |
|---------|---------|------|
| **WhatsApp** | First contact + follow-up + closing | Default. Most owners answer here. |
| **Instagram DM** | First contact when no WA is public | Design-forward brands; softer opener. |
| **Live demo link** | The "wow" moment | After they reply "show me". |
| **Loom / video walkthrough** | Differentiation + async sell | Attach with the link; proves you did the work. |
| **PDF** | Only if they explicitly ask | Avoid — a PDF hides the motion that sells it. |
| **Email** | Contract + presupuesto | Last step only, once they're warm. |

**Golden rule:** Never lead with the price. Lead with the *demo*. Price comes after they want it.

## 5. Objection handling

| Objection | Response (Rioplatense) |
|-----------|------------------------|
| **"No tengo presupuesto ahora."** | `Tranqui, no es para ahora mismo. La idea es que lo veas y, cuando arranques la temporada / tengas el flujo, lo largan. El presupuesto lo dejo armado y lo congelo.` |
| **"¿Cuánto sale?" (too early)** | `Te lo paso recién cuando veas el demo, porque el precio depende de qué tantas secciones quieras. Pero es un numero razonable para una web corporativa de alta gama, no es agencia grande.` |
| **"Ya tengo web."** | ` Perfecto, la vi 👀 — por eso pensé en mostrarte esto: se nota que el producto es de otra liga y el sitio no lo acompaña todavía. No es rehacer todo, es darle la imagen que ya tenés en obra.` |
| **"Somos muy chicos para esto."** | ` Justo para los que están creciendo sirve más: el sitio te hace parecer el que ya es referencia. Varios arrancaron chicos y el canal web les trajo los primeros grandes clientes.` |
| **"Mandame presupuesto por mail."** | ` ` Dale, te lo mando. Pero primero ¿viste el demo? Porque el presupuesto tiene más sentido cuando ves lo que incluye. Igual te lo paso ahora.` |
| **"Lo charlo con mi socio/mujer."** | ` ` Obvio, pasale el link también: [LINK]. Si quieren, armo una videollamada de 15 min para los dos y les muestro la onda en vivo.` |
| **"Suena caro / nosotros hacemos todo barato."** | ` ` Entiendo, pero esto no compite en precio, compite en que el sitio cierra ventas solo. Una web barata que no convierte sale más cara a la larga.` |

## 6. How to present the mockup (format guide)

| Format | Best for | How |
|--------|----------|-----|
| **Live link** (GitHub Pages) | The default "wow" | Send the URL. Say "navegalo". It shows motion/scroll — the thing that sells it. |
| **Loom video (2–3 min)** | Async differentiation | Record: hero → comparison → WhatsApp CTA → form. Narrate in Spanish, calm. Proves effort. |
| **PDF** | Only if forced | Export 4–5 screens (hero, comparison, projects, form). Note: loses motion — weak. |
| **Live walkthrough (Meet)** | Hot lead / 2 decision-makers | Share screen, drive the scroll, pause on comparison + guarantee. Close on the call. |

**Tip:** Always send **link + Loom together**. The link = they explore; the Loom = you lead the story.

## 10. What NOT to do (red flags)

- ❌ Lead with price or a formal PDF proposal. Locks you into "commodity" mode.
- ❌ Send the demo to 50 people at once (looks spammy). One-to-one, named, specific compliment.
- ❌ Over-promise ("primero en Google", "garantizado"). Argentine buyers smell it.
- ❌ Use "usted" in WA — use "vos"/"che" lightly; match their energy.
- ❌ Forget to screenshot *their* current site — it's your sharpest contrast argument.
- ❌ Close only by WA for the contract — send the presupuesto by email too, for record.
